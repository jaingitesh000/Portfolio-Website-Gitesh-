Case Studies

Quest+ Learning Experience Redesign

Revamped the Quest+ learning platform to improve student engagement, simplify navigation, and create a clearer learning experience. The redesign focused on usability, progress tracking, and assessment feedback, leading to higher engagement and a stronger foundation for future gamification features.

Teach Portal & Mobile App — Classroom Management Experience

Redesigned the Teach Portal and Mobile App to streamline classroom management, simplify assessment workflows, and improve communication between teachers and students. The platform focused on reducing operational friction for educators while creating a more structured and accessible learning experience across web and mobile devices.

Group Masterclass (Fluent Life) — Learning Community Experience

Designed the Group Masterclass experience for Fluent Life to create a more interactive, structured, and engaging learning environment for users participating in cohort-based programs. The platform focused on improving learner participation, community interaction, session accessibility, and overall learning consistency across the masterclass journey.
