Quest+ Learning Experience Redesign

Revamped the Quest+ learning platform to improve student engagement, simplify navigation, and create a clearer learning experience. The redesign focused on usability, progress tracking, and assessment feedback, leading to higher engagement and a stronger foundation for future gamification features.

Overview

Quest+ is an education platform used by partnered schools for coursework, assessments, quizzes, and progress tracking. While the platform had strong educational content, the overall user experience lacked clarity and engagement, especially for students using the platform daily.

The goal of this revamp was not just to modernize the interface, but to improve usability, reduce cognitive friction, and increase meaningful engagement.

The Problem

The platform had multiple engagement and usability gaps:

Students often didn't know what to do next
Progress tracking felt overwhelming and difficult to understand
Core actions were buried under cluttered layouts
Quiz feedback focused on scores instead of learning
Existing gamification elements lacked depth and adoption
As a result, users were visiting the platform but not engaging with it meaningfully over time.

Goal

The primary objective was to improve engagement quality and make the learning journey more intuitive and motivating.

Key focus areas
Simplify navigation and action paths
Improve clarity across core flows
Make progress easier to understand
Create stronger feedback loops for learning
Build a scalable UX foundation for future gamification

Key Product Decisions

1. Simplified the Learning Entry Point

The homepage had multiple competing actions, making it difficult for students to understand where to start. I restructured the experience around a clearer primary learning path, with stronger hierarchy and more focused entry points.
This reduced the amount of decision-making required before students could begin learning.

Result

Students could navigate faster and interact more consistently with learning content.

2. Shifted From Content to Guided Learning

The original experience was organized mainly around chapters and activities, but students still had to figure out how to move through them. I reorganized lessons, topics, and assessments around the actual learning journey.
This made the experience feel more guided rather than simply presenting students with a list of content.

Result

Students could more easily identify relevant activities, understand their learning status, and move between lessons, practice, and assessments.

3. Reframed Progress as Guidance

Progress was previously presented through percentages and completion data, but it didn't provide much context around what students should focus on. I introduced stronger visual hierarchy and clearer subject-level insights to make progress easier to interpret.
The intention was to make progress feel encouraging and actionable rather than like a static report.

  • •Clearer feedback
  • •Answer review
  • •Topic-level understanding
  • •Visual performance summaries
Result

Students could quickly understand their performance, identify learning gaps, and focus on areas that needed more attention.

4. Made Assessment Feedback More Actionable

Quiz results were primarily focused on the final score, which limited the learning value after an assessment. I redesigned the experience to connect performance with answer review, explanations, and clearer performance breakdowns.
This created a more natural path from seeing the result to understanding mistakes and learning from them.

  • •Quiz contests
  • •Rewards
  • •Leaderboard systems
  • •Interactive learning loops
Result

The assessment experience became more reflective and learning-oriented instead of simply ending with a score.

5. Turned Assessment Data Into Insights

I introduced a consolidated performance view that brings together scores, topic-level performance, strengths, and areas for improvement. This gives students a clearer understanding of their learning beyond individual quiz results.
The experience also makes the information easier for parents to understand and use when supporting their child.

Result

Students and parents could understand performance beyond marks, identify strengths and gaps, and use the insights to support ongoing improvement.
